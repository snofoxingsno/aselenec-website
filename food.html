Food

Honest notes
on places worth eating.

A record of restaurants that are actually good — and a few that aren't. No sponsorships. No star chasing. Just the food.

Cha Chaan Teng 4.0 / 10

瑞士咖啡室

Hong Kong

瑞士汁雞腿撈麵 — Swiss sauce chicken thigh lo mein. Pretty shit. Sauce was cloying, noodles overcooked and mushy. Chicken thigh was fine on its own but didn't save it. Not going back for this one.

Japanese 9.1 / 10

Neighborhood Sushi

Wan Chai, Hong Kong

Omakase that punches above its price point. The chef does something interesting with aged fish — nothing gimmicky, just controlled. Worth the booking wait.

Cantonese 8.4 / 10

Yung Kee

Central, Hong Kong

A classic that earns its reputation. The roast goose is as good as advertised. Go at lunch to avoid the tourist dinner crowd — the room is different.

Photo
Thai 8.7 / 10

Chachawan

Sheung Wan, Hong Kong

Northeastern Thai done right. The larb here has actual heat and proper fermented funk — not the sanitised version. Loud room but you don't notice after the first round.
